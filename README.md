# AI Adoption & Support Kit

An independent proposal by Shorouk for how Factorial could get SMB customers using its AI assistant every week, prove the hours saved, and route support so nothing gets lost.

**Live page:** https://shorouk9.github.io/factorial-ai-adoption-kit/

## What's inside

- **Four-week activation plan**: enter headcount, HR team size, market and modules; the plan climbs from employees asking, to employees acting, to managers, to HR's heaviest work.
- **Hours-saved calculator**: editable volumes and minutes per task, in hours and euros, ready for a day-30 review.
- **Adoption health check**: six usage signals turn into on track, needs a nudge or at risk, with the next three actions.
- **Support triage**: paste a customer message to see the category, priority, team, target time and a first reply. A simple keyword router that shows the routing rules.
- **Support model and measures.**
